Questions

How long does a website take?

Most small business sites take two to four weeks from the first call to launch. Stores and larger sites take longer; you get a timeline with your quote.

How much does it cost?

Every project gets a fixed quote after the free call. You know the full price before any work starts, with no surprise extras.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain, content and code belong to you, and we can hand everything over at any time.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. We can set up simple editing for text and images, or make the changes for you as part of hosting & care.

I already have a domain or an old website.

That's fine. We work with your existing domain and move over any content worth keeping.
